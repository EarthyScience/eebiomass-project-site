With ESA's pioneering Biomass mission poised to revolutionize how we measure global forest carbon stocks and explore our planet’s subsurface layers, the global Calibration and Validation (Cal/Val) community is coming together.   

We are hosting a two-part webinar series this October to showcase the incredible scope of accepted Cal/Val projects.From the dense tropical rainforests of the Amazon to the massive ice shelves of Antarctica, discover how researchers are preparing to unlock the power of P-band radar.  

👇 Save the Dates and Register:  

📅 Part 1: Measuring the World's Forests: Cal/Val Projects for Biomass, Structure, and Disturbance   

    Date & Time: <b>October 22 | 11:00 – 13:00 CET</b>  
    The Focus: How we map aboveground biomass (AGB), canopy height, and forest disturbances across the globe.  

📅 Part 2: Beyond the Trees: Technical Calibration, Ionosphere Corrections, and Secondary Mission Objectives

    Date & Time: <b>October 29 | 15:00 – 16:30 CET</b>  
    The Focus: Moving beyond forests to investigate system performance and exciting secondary mission objectives, including subsurface desert imaging, glacier and ice shelf dynamics, and groundwater and soil moisture detection.
